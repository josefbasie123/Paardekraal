Welcome To

Paardekraal Kliphuis

Namaqualand is regarded as a biodiversity hotspot boasting the richest succulent flora on earth. Paardekraal Kliphuis puts you right in the heart of this remarkable landscape — a self-catering stone cottage on a working farm between Springbok and Kamieskroon.

Built from the same rough granite that litters the surrounding hillside, the cottage keeps you cool in summer and warm on cold winter nights, with a fully equipped kitchen, a built-in fireplace, and an en-suite bedroom with a woodstove and a view of the rising sun.

Location Overview

Namaqualand, Northern Cape

Paardekraal Kliphuis sits on the R539 Droëdap road, on a working farm between Springbok and Kamieskroon. The rhythm here is slow, and it is easy to breathe.

The road in requires reasonable ground clearance — please check with us before you travel if you are unsure about your vehicle. Close to Namaqua National Park and the Skilpad Wildflower Reserve.
