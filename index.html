The Cottage

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

The cottage sits low against the land, at the foot of a mountain. Built from the same rough granite that litters the surrounding hillside, sun-bleached and mortared by hands that knew this landscape intimately.

The veranda faces North-East to catch the winter sun, with a braai area to the one side and a simple wooden bench where you can watch the extraordinary light change over the plains.

When you step inside, the thick walls announce themselves — keeping you cool in summer and holding warmth on cold winter nights. The fully equipped kitchen occupies one corner, with a built-in fireplace for cooking or keeping warm, leading through to the comfortable couch in the lounge and glass doors that open onto the landscape.

The bedroom with an en-suite bathroom is unpretentious but deeply comfortable, with a woodstove and a view of the rising sun.

Birdwatching

This semi-arid land is one of the world's biodiversity hotspots and the bird community reflects this richness. A mosaic of micro-habitats support a surprising diversity of species.

Namaqualand however is not a place of easy ticks and dense flocks. It rewards the traveler who slows down, tunes their ear to the silence and learns to read a landscape that hides its riches in plain sight.

Photography

Paardekraal has a landscape of rolling plains, granite kopjes (rocky outcrops) and dry riverbeds under an enormous sky. For most of the year it is a study in ochres, greys and dusty greens — thorny shrubs, succulents and sun-bleached rock.

The light here is extraordinary: low-angled in the morning and evening, and intensely directional. But Namaqualand's most famous trick is its spring transformation. Between late July and September it erupts into a spectacular wildflower display — carpets of orange, yellow, purple and white.

Cycling

The road network is any gravel cyclist's quiet dream. The gradients on the plains are gentle and generally rideable on a gravel bike or mountain bike.

The scenery shifts slowly as you move through it. Cycling gives you something driving never can: the smell of the shrubs, the sound of wind and larks, and the full physical experience of the scale of the landscape.

Hiking

The hiking landscape consists of open plains, seemingly featureless from a distance but alive underfoot with quartz pebbles, dwarf succulents, lizard tracks and plants that have learned to survive on almost nothing.

Rounded, boulder-strewn rocky outcrops punctuate the plains — cool shade, surprising plant life clinging to the cracks, and panoramic views from the top across a landscape that seems to go on forever.

Breakfast on the Plains

There is no agenda. The nearest town is an hour away. Your phone has no signal. The bread is freshly baked, the coffee is hot, and the world in front of you is ancient, enormous and unhurried.

Breakfast on the Namaqualand plains is less a meal than a quiet ceremony — a way of greeting a landscape that has been here long before you and will remain long after you leave.

Location Overview

Namaqualand, Northern Cape

Paardekraal Kliphuis sits on the R539 Droëdap road, on a working farm between Springbok and Kamieskroon. The rhythm here is slow, and it is easy to breathe.

The road in requires reasonable ground clearance — please check with us before you travel if you are unsure about your vehicle.
